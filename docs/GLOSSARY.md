# Glossary

Research use only.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bedaquiline | An antibiotic used in tuberculosis care. |
| Isolate | A recorded tuberculosis sample in the study. |
| Phenotype | The resistance or susceptibility label recorded by a laboratory. |
| Catalogue call | The call produced by applying recorded catalogue rules to a sample. |
| Out-of-fold | A result from a fold that left the displayed isolate out of training. |
| Allele fraction | The estimated share of reads supporting the variant. |
| Firm call | A resistant or susceptible catalogue call. |
| Lab referral | An unresolved call that was deferred for laboratory testing. |
| Sensitivity | The share of resistant isolates called resistant, among the cases specified by the metric. |
| Specificity | The share of susceptible isolates called susceptible, among the cases specified by the metric. |
| PPV / NPV | The share of resistant or susceptible calls, respectively, expected to match their phenotype at a stated prevalence. |
| Wilson interval | An uncertainty interval for an observed proportion. |
| ROC AUC | A summary of how the scores order resistant and susceptible isolates across thresholds. |
