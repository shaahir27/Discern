Assay Blind-Spot Analysis

Sequencing-Assay & tNGS Panel Coverage Audit

Commercial and public health targeted NGS (tNGS) panels sequence subsets of bedaquiline-associated genes. Treating unsequenced loci as wild-type introduces silent false-susceptible risk and disables the mmpL5 epistatic pump veto.

⚠️ Diagnostic Coverage Caveat:

Genomic locus coverage represents a theoretical upper bound on resistance detection. Real-world sensitivity is further constrained by amplicon sequencing depth, low-frequency subclonal heteroresistance, and Nanopore/Illumina homopolymer calling noise (Hall et al., eLife 2024; Ismail et al., AAC 2025).

Cohort Reference Breakdown (N = 866 BDQ-R Isolates)

Across 11,868 isolates in the reference cohort (CRyPTIC + NICD), 866 were phenotypically bedaquiline resistant. Among these, 729 isolates (84.2%) harbored mutations in established core loci (Rv0678, atpE, or pepQ), while 137 isolates (15.8%) carried no core-gene variant (invisible resistance).

  • Standard Deeplex Myc-TB & ICMR-NIRT MinION (Rv0678-only): Cover 88.5% of known-locus resistant strains. They miss 11.5% of known resistance drivers (atpE ~7.0%, pepQ ~5.8%), and cannot evaluate the mmpL5 epistatic veto.
  • AmPORE-TB (Rv0678 + atpE): Covers 94.2% of known-locus resistant strains, but misses pepQ and cannot evaluate mmpL5 epistatic inactivation.
  • Deeplex Myc-TB XL & WGS (All 5 genes): Deliver 100% coverage of known resistance loci and active epistatic veto auditing.
