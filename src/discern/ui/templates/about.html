About Discern

An offline research archive for evaluating bedaquiline resistance in tuberculosis.

How the archive works

Replay opens a recorded isolate with its saved catalogue call, laboratory phenotype, and out-of-fold model probability. Evidence cards show the observed carrier counts by source and their uncertainty.

Review and reproduce

Use Performance to explore the experiment registry and follow the interpretation and caveats in the model card. The review queue organizes unresolved examples for inspection.

Data handling

The app reads bundled results from this workspace. CSV review accepts recorded replay IDs and processes the list in memory.
