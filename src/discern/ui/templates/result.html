Biological mechanism & clinical guidance

{% for w in record.warnings %} {% if w == "EPISTATIC_PUMP_INACTIVATION" or (record.tier_a and record.tier_a.epistatic_veto_applied) %}
⚡ Epistatic Pump Inactivation Veto (WHO Catalogue v3 / TB-Profiler v6.6.5 Rule)

This isolate carries an Rv0678 mutation, but the efflux pump gene mmpL5 contains an allele-frequency linked inactivating frameshift or nonsense mutation. Because the MmpS5-MmpL5 efflux pump protein cannot assemble, bedaquiline cannot be extruded. In locked S1 cross-validation (E16 audit), 95.1% (39/41) of vetoed isolates were phenotypically susceptible, while 2 resistant isolates were safely referred for phenotypic testing.

{% elif w == "HETERORESISTANCE_SUBCLONAL" %}
⚠️ Subclonal Heteroresistance Detected (0.10 ≤ FRS < 0.75)

Contributing variant is present as a minority allele. Heteroresistant sub-clones often test susceptible on 14-day microtiter plates (UKMYC, ECOFF 0.25 mg/L) but grow out as resistant during extended liquid culture (MGIT960) (Nimmo et al., Lancet Microbe 2020; Snobre et al., AAC 2024; Schön et al., AAC 2025). Phenotypic DST confirmation in liquid culture is advised.
